Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to Yakmukja (약먹자), provided by JTJ. The Android package name is com.jtj.yakmeokja.

Developer: JTJLast updated: November 19, 2026jtaejuwork@gmail.com

Medication information is stored on your device. No registration or sign-in is required, and JTJ does not operate a server that stores medication data. Google services process information for advertising and purchases. Android backup and device transfer are covered under “Retention, backup, and deletion.”

Information stored on your device

InformationDetails and purpose
Medication and reminder settingsMedication names you enter, dose times and selected time zones, pill appearance, reminder enablement, alarm sound references, and sound and vibration settings. These are used to display your medicines and provide reminders.
Dose confirmation stateThe current state needed for “Taken” and undo actions. The app determines the state through the next dosing interval; it does not create a separate, long-term, dated medication history.
App preferencesWhether you have finished the first-use guide. The app uses device language and the default time zone for display and initial settings. You can select a different time zone for each medicine.
Purchase verificationPurchase information and signed receipts returned by Google Play are processed to verify and restore ad removal. A verified receipt copy is saved on the device, as explained below.

The app does not send medication names, dose schedules, or dose confirmation states to a JTJ server or include them in ad requests. These details may reveal health information. Take care when sharing your device with others.

The app writes billing connection and product-query status, product identifiers, and returned prices to device diagnostic logs. It does not automatically upload these logs to a developer server.

Reminders and device features

Reminders are scheduled locally using Android alarms and notifications. They can be rescheduled after a reboot, app update, or clock or time-zone change. Reminder settings use device-protected storage so scheduling can resume before the first unlock after reboot. The app does not use a developer-operated push notification server.

  • Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alarms, background alarm playback, vibration, and wake functions support medication reminders. You can manage the relevant permissions in Android settings.
  • Medication names, dose times, and time zones may appear in notifications and on the lock screen. Adjust Android’s lock-screen notification settings to limit this display.
  • Accelerometer readings are used only to animate pills in the bottle. The readings are not saved to files or transmitted.
  • The app plays a sound chosen through the system ringtone picker. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or GPS location permissions. Selecting a time zone is not GPS tracking.

AdMob advertising and privacy choices

The free version may show Google AdMob banner ads. Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, advertising IDs, and app set IDs for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Account-related identifiers may also be processed where applicable. IP addresses can be used to estimate approximate location. Details are available in Google’s SDK disclosure.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks consent status and presents privacy forms when required. When the SDK requires a privacy-options entry, “Ad privacy settings” appears in the side menu. Available choices are managed in that form; advertising ID controls are also available in Android settings.

Once ad-removal ownership is verified, the app stops new banner requests and removes the ad area. Banners are also hidden on alarm and dose-confirmation screens. Buying ad removal does not delete data Google already holds.

Ad removal through Google Play

Ad removal is a one-time, non-consumable Google Play purchase. Google Play handles payment and payment methods. JTJ’s app does not ask for or store card numbers or payment-method passwords.

The app queries information Google Play returns, including purchase products, status, tokens, and signatures. It checks receipt signatures and purchase acknowledgement before applying ad removal. A verified receipt and signature are kept in Android’s no-backup storage for offline ownership checks. The app does not send receipts to a developer-operated verification server.

“Restore purchases” and app launch/resume recheck current purchases with Google Play. Clearing app data does not cancel the Google Play purchase; it can be restored with the purchasing account. Refunds and purchase-history management follow Google Play’s processes.

Retention, backup, and deletion

Medication settings stay on the device until you change or delete them. The saved dose state is used to determine the current dosing interval; the stored value is not automatically erased when that interval ends. The receipt copy supports ad-removal verification and is removed when app data is cleared or a later successful purchase query confirms there is no entitlement.

  1. Delete a medicine: Open its confirmation dialog, select “Settings,” then choose and confirm deletion. Its stored information and dose state are removed and its scheduled alarms are cancelled.
  2. Remove local app data: Use Android Settings → Apps → Yakmukja → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. This removes locally stored medication data, preferences, and receipt copies. Setting names vary by device.
  3. Data held by Google: Uninstalling does not itself delete Google’s purchase history, advertising-related information, or system backups. Manage these through the relevant service, account, and backup controls.

The app allows Android backup and device transfer. Depending on the device, operating system, and user settings, some app data may be included. JTJ does not provide separate cloud synchronization. Receipt copies and the guide-completion marker use no-backup storage. Backup creation, restoration, deletion, and retention depend on Android and the backup provider; JTJ cannot remotely delete those copies.

Google service retention and deletion follow Google’s Privacy Policy. The app cannot set retention periods for, or delete, all information Google holds.

Protection and external services

Medication data is stored in Android’s app-private storage. The app does not currently add its own file encryption or a separate app lock. Google states that data collected by Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Also manage your device lock and notification visibility.

The app uses Google Mobile Ads 25.5.0, Google UMP 4.0.0, and Google Play Billing 9.1.0. Google’s policies and settings govern its data processing, sharing, and international processing.
