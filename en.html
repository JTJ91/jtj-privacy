Chaenggija Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to Chaenggija (챙기자), provided by JTJ. The Android package name is com.jtj.chaenggija.

Developer: JTJLast updated: November 25, 2026jtaejuwork@gmail.com

Chaenggija saves a selected reference time zone for each reminder and uses it to schedule one-time or repeating reminders, including when travel changes your device’s time zone. Reminder information is stored on your device. There is no app registration, sign-in, or developer-operated reminder synchronization server. Google services collect and process information for advertising and ad-removal purchases. Android backup and device transfer are covered under “Retention, backup, and deletion.”

Information stored on your device

InformationDetails and purpose

These support scheduling and the display of reminder icons, details, and alarm screens.

The app writes billing connection and product-query status, product identifiers, and returned prices to device diagnostic logs. It does not automatically upload these logs to a developer server.

Reminders and device features

Reminders are scheduled locally using Android alarms and notifications and the IANA time zone saved for each reminder. That reference time zone stays unchanged when the device’s time zone changes. Reminders can be rescheduled after a reboot, app update, or clock or time-zone change. Reminder settings use device-protected storage so scheduling can resume before the first unlock after reboot. The app does not use a developer-operated push notification server.

  • Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alarms, background alarm playback, vibration, and wake functions support daily reminders. You can manage the relevant permissions in Android settings.
  • Reminder titles, reminder times, and time zones may appear in notifications and on the lock screen. Adjust Android’s lock-screen notification settings to limit this display.
  • Accelerometer readings are used only while the app animates the tilting, shaking, and collisions of icons inside the selected case. The readings are not saved to files or transmitted.
  • The app plays a sound chosen through the system ringtone picker. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or GPS location permissions. Selecting a time zone is not GPS tracking.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free version may show a Google AdMob banner in a dedicated area at the bottom of the home screen. A divider and spacing separate it from the add button. Banners are hidden on alarm screens and while editing or viewing reminder details. Development builds use Google demo ads.

Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares the following information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. This external processing by the advertising SDK can occur even though the developer does not operate a reminder server.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks consent status and presents privacy forms as required by the region and service configuration. When the SDK requires a privacy-options entry, “Ad privacy settings” appears in the side menu. Available choices are managed in that form; advertising ID controls are also available in Android settings. Some information may still be processed for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention after advertising preferences change.

Once ad-removal ownership is verified, the app stops new banner requests and removes the ad area. Banners are also hidden on alarm and reminder-completion screens. Buying ad removal does not delete data Google already holds.

Ad removal through Google Play

Ad removal is a one-time, non-consumable Google Play purchase. Google Play handles payment and payment methods. JTJ’s app does not ask for or store card numbers or payment-method passwords.

The app queries information Google Play returns, including purchase products, status, tokens, and signatures. It checks receipt signatures and purchase acknowledgement before applying ad removal. A verified receipt and signature are kept in Android’s no-backup storage for offline ownership checks. The app does not send receipts to a developer-operated verification server.

“Restore purchases” and app launch/resume recheck current purchases with Google Play. Clearing app data does not cancel the Google Play purchase; it can be restored with the purchasing account. Refunds and purchase-history management follow Google Play’s processes.

Retention, backup, and deletion

Reminder settings and delivery states stay on the device until you change or delete them. Marking a one-time reminder as complete deletes that reminder and its associated local history and cancels its scheduled alarm. Repeating-reminder completion state is used to determine the current reminder interval; the stored value is not automatically erased when that interval ends. The receipt copy supports ad-removal verification and is removed when app data is cleared or a later successful purchase query confirms there is no entitlement.

  1. Delete a reminder: Tap the reminder, select “Delete” in its details, and confirm. Its stored information, completion state, and associated history are removed, and its scheduled alarms are cancelled.
  2. Remove local app data: Use Android Settings → Apps → Chaenggija → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. This removes locally stored reminder data, preferences, and receipt copies. Setting names vary by device.
  3. Data held by Google: Uninstalling does not itself delete Google’s purchase history, advertising-related information, or system backups. Manage these through the relevant service, account, and backup controls.

The app allows Android backup and device transfer. Depending on the device, operating system, and user settings, some app data may be included. JTJ does not provide separate cloud synchronization. Receipt copies and the guide-completion marker use no-backup storage. Backup creation, restoration, deletion, and retention depend on Android and the backup provider; JTJ cannot remotely delete those copies.

Google service retention and deletion follow Google’s Privacy Policy. The app cannot set retention periods for, or delete, all information Google holds.

Protection and external services

Reminder data is stored in Android’s app-private storage. The app does not currently add its own file encryption or a separate app lock. Google states that data collected by Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Also manage your device lock and notification visibility.

The app uses Google Mobile Ads 25.5.0, Google UMP 4.0.0, and Google Play Billing 9.1.0. Google’s policies and settings govern its data processing, sharing, and international processing.

Contact and policy updates

Contact JTJ at jtaejuwork@gmail.com with privacy questions or questions about deletion. “Contact us” opens an email draft with the recipient and subject; it does not automatically attach reminder data or receipts. Email is sent only when you choose to send it.
